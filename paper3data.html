How to Use This Table

This table shows which maths topics appear most frequently in past papers and their relative importance. Use it to guide where to focus your study time for the GCSE.

Understanding the Columns

Branch
The main area of mathematics (e.g., Algebra, Geometry and Measures)
Topic
The specific topic within that branch
Marks
Total marks allocated to this topic across all papers
Frequency
Number of times this topic appeared in the papers
Average Marks
Average marks per appearance (calculated as: Marks ÷ Frequency)
Likelihood of Appearance
Probability this topic will appear in a future paper (calculated as: Frequency ÷ Number of Papers)
Relative Importance
Combined score showing how essential this topic is (calculated as: Average Marks × Likelihood of Appearance)

Study Strategy

Sort by Relative Importance: Topics with higher values should be your priority—they're likely to appear and are worth significant marks.

Don't Skip Low-Importance Topics: Even topics with lower scores are examinable and may be combined with higher-priority content in questions.
