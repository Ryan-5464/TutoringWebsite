Column Guide

  • Branch groups each topic into a broader area of the specification.
  • Topic names the specific curriculum area tracked in the dataset.
  • Marks is the total number of marks awarded to that topic across all papers included in this dataset.
  • Frequency records the total number of times the topic appears across the full paper set.
  • Average Marks shows the mean number of marks available each time the topic appears, calculated as total marks divided by frequency.
  • Average Number of Appearances shows the mean number of appearances per paper across the dataset.
  • Number of Papers states how many papers were included when compiling the topic data.
